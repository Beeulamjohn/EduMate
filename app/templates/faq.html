Admissions & Registration

Enquiries, admission and student accounts

Browse the available courses on the SciPy Technologies website, choose a suitable course and submit your enquiry or registration form. The admission team can guide you through counselling, course selection and admission confirmation.

You may need to provide your name, date of birth, mobile number, email, address, educational qualification, college details and selected course. A photograph, identity proof and educational documents may also be required.

Yes. The system is designed to generate a unique student ID and admission number after the required registration and admission steps are completed.

Once your student account and admission records are available, sign in to the student portal to view your admission details. You can also contact the admission team for assistance.

Courses & Learning Management

Course types, batches and online learning

The platform supports technical and non-technical courses, short-term and long-term training, diploma programmes and internship-related learning.

Examples include Python, Django, Full Stack Development, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Digital Marketing and Business Management.

Courses can be configured for online, offline, hybrid, live, recorded or self-paced learning, depending on the programme offered.

The LMS is designed to provide authorised students with learning materials such as videos, PDFs, notes, assignments, quizzes and other course resources through the student portal.

Access depends on your enrolment and the permissions configured for your course.

Yes. The planned student dashboard includes course progress and academic information. Attendance records can be maintained for courses, batches, internships and other academic activities.

The academic team can manage course batches, start and end dates, timetables, trainers and student allocations through the academic management module.

Fees & Payments

Fee structure, instalments and receipts

The fee management module supports instalment-based payments. The configured fee plan can track the instalment amount, due date, amount paid, pending balance and payment status.

Available instalment options depend on the fee plan approved for your course.

The system supports recording payments made through cash, bank transfer, UPI, card, cheque and payment gateways. Online gateway availability depends on the integrations enabled by SciPy Technologies.

After a payment is verified and recorded, the system is designed to generate a payment receipt. Sign in to your student portal and open the payments section to access available receipts.

Your student dashboard is designed to show the total fee, amount paid and remaining balance. Contact the accounts team if your payment or balance information appears incorrect.

The system specification includes fee-due and overdue notifications. Depending on the configured services, reminders may be sent through the student portal, email, SMS or WhatsApp.

Projects, Examinations & Certificates

Project tracking, results and verification

Yes. The project management module is designed to support project registration, guide allocation, document uploads, progress tracking, project reviews, evaluation and completion status.

Eligible students can follow the configured examination registration process. This may include eligibility verification, exam registration, payment of the examination fee and hall ticket generation.

Once results have been published and made available, you can view your marks, percentage, grade and result status through the student portal, according to the configured result access rules.

The system specification includes downloadable PDF hall tickets, marksheets and results. These documents will be available when they have been generated and published for your examination.

The certificate management module is designed to support unique certificate IDs and QR-based verification for configured certificates. Verification availability depends on the certificate service being implemented.

Internship & Career Support

Internship progress and placement services

The platform supports short-term and long-term internships. Example durations in the specification range from 7 days to 60 days for short-term internships and 3 to 12 months for long-term internships.

The internship module is designed to track attendance, tasks, projects, reviews, mentor feedback, performance and completion status.

Yes. The planned placement module supports job opportunities, student applications, interview tracking and placement status. Available services depend on the placement features enabled by the organisation.

For admission enquiries, contact the admission team. For fees, contact the accounts team. For course access, projects or examinations, contact the appropriate academic team.

You can also use the contact details provided on the official SciPy Technologies website.
